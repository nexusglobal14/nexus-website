About Nexus Pakistan

Nexus Pakistan is a small project of Nexus Global Information Technology. It gives you one quick page to see the latest Urdu headlines, what Pakistan is searching for on Google today and the current USD and EUR rates in rupees.

What we do

We collect public headline feeds and daily trend lists, rebuild the page whenever they change, and link every item back to its original source. We do not republish article text.

What we do not do

We are not a newsroom. We do not write or verify the news that appears here, so please read the original publisher for the full story. Exchange rates are indicative only.
