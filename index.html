About this page

Nexus Pakistan is a simple daily dashboard that brings together three things people in Pakistan check often: the latest news headlines, the topics trending on Google Search in Pakistan, and the US dollar and euro exchange rates against the Pakistani rupee.

Where the information comes from

  • Headlines come from the public RSS feed of BBC Urdu. We show the headline and link to the original article, which opens on the BBC website. We do not copy article text.
  • Trends are the daily trending searches for Pakistan from Google Trends. Each item links to its Google Trends page. Search volume is shown as Google reports it, for example 50K+.
  • Exchange rates are fetched live in your browser from the open.er-api.com service.

How often it updates

Headlines and trends are refreshed regularly and the page is rebuilt each time. The time of the last update is shown at the top of the page. Exchange rates refresh every minute while the page is open.

Limits and disclaimer

Headlines are the work of their original publishers and Nexus Pakistan is not responsible for their content. Trending topics only show what people are searching for, not whether something is true. Exchange rates are indicative and should not be used for financial decisions without checking with your bank or exchange company.
